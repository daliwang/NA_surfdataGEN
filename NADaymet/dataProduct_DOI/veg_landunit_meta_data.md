## DATA & FILE OVERVIEW

1. **File List:**  
   surfdata.Daymet_NA.nalcms.1km.2d.VegMapLandUnitTemp.c260524.nc

2. **Relationship between files:**  
   Version `c260524` supersedes `c251230.nc` (initial public release). The current product incorporates three pipeline corrections applied and rebuilt together: (1) **glacier handling**—NALCMS class 19 (Snow/Ice) is mapped to a separate glacier land unit (`PCT_GLACIER`) rather than PFT0 in the vegetation stack; (2) **climate-informed PFT splits**—Daymet mean-temperature-of-the-coldest-month (MTCO) rules split selected NALCMS classes, including an **18 °C threshold** for tropical evergreen (class 3) into warm broadleaf evergreen temperate trees (PFT4) and cool broadleaf evergreen boreal trees (PFT5), with `AvgTempColdMonth` written to the final NetCDF; (3) **inland open water**—the initial release excluded 1 km cells where `na_mask=0` from class-18 aggregation, leaving incomplete `PCT_LAKE` over inland lakes. `c260524` corrects all three upstream and reruns the full workflow: glacier and lake as separate land units, MTCO-based PFT assignment, extension of `na_mask` by 228,169 class-18 centre cells with re-counted water-class layers, initialization of newly opened cells, and guarding of PFT totals on water-dominated cells, yielding a self-consistent NetCDF without downstream post-processing.

## METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION

1. **Description of methods used for collection/generation of data:**  
   The vegetation map was generated by the scripts in nalcms2daymet_script directory in the repository at `https://github.com/daliwang/NALCMS2Daymet_ELM`. 

2. **Methods for processing the data:**  
   The methods are described in `methods_nalcms_to_daymet.tex` and `methods_nadaymet.tex` in the NALCMS2Daymet_ELM repository at `https://github.com/daliwang/NALCMS2Daymet_ELM`. Those documents cover NALCMS reprojection and class counting (including the active-domain mask and inland open-water correction for `c260524`), MTCO-based PFT assignment (including the 18 °C tropical-evergreen split and glacier land-unit handling), and final surfdata assembly.
